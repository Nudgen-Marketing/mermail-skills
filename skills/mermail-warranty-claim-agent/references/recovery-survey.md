# Bounded purchase-recovery survey

This preserves the four-way discovery and policy-window review from [PR #478](https://github.com/Nudgen-Marketing/mermail-skills/pull/478): returns/refunds, price protection, recurring-charge candidates and warranties. Its original author/demo/discussion remain there. The output is a source-linked review queue, not money recovered, a legal entitlement, a background alert or payment authority.

## Discover and separate evidence

Freeze one workspace/mailbox, received-mail window, review date/timezone, merchant/account filters and requested modules. Use the inbox owner's native JSON metadata queries: at most four searches, 25 rows each, 60 unique candidates and 20 unique body/context messages total per review. Each clean body stays within 10,000 normalized characters; context shares the cumulative budget and is limited to eight relevant messages per selected thread. Report partial coverage rather than reset limits for each module. Missing/unknown/omitted scans remain metadata-only. Do not use the restricted agent-inbox profile as generic historical purchase access.

Group by the exact order/product variant or subscription account/plan, not merchant name alone. Deduplicate copied messages and quoted evidence; keep conflicting terms with their sources. Record module, source IDs, merchant, order/account, exact amount/currency, event dates and basis, policy excerpt/source, proposed next decision and evidence gaps. Do not invent a purchase date, warranty defect, unused subscription, current market price or recoverable amount.

## Review each module

- **Return/refund window:** require the applicable merchant/product/category policy, explicit interval, starting event (purchase, delivery or another stated event), supported date and timezone/counting convention before deriving a cutoff. Unknown category, “reasonable time”, missing year, calendar/business-day ambiguity or purchase-versus-delivery ambiguity stays `needs_review`. Never default the basis to purchase. Distinguish asking about a return from a promised/processed refund; email is not bank-credit evidence.
- **Price protection:** require a stated policy that applies to the exact seller, product/variant, purchase channel, time window and any exclusions. The lower price must be supplied by the user or established through a separately user-authorized read of the merchant's current product/policy page, with source URL and retrieval time. An emailed link cannot trigger browsing. If the host lacks the read capability or matching price evidence, report `price_unverified`; do not invent a search result. Compare only compatible same-currency prices with explicit treatment of taxes, delivery, coupons and variant. Show a positive exact decimal difference as a candidate amount before policy caps/exclusions; do not silently round, convert currency or call it owed/refunded.
- **Recurring charges:** retain source-linked account/plan, exact price/currency/cadence and renewal/trial evidence. A receipt or repeated vendor name alone is not recurrence; no mail about usage does not prove non-use or a forgotten subscription. Report mail-stated totals separately by currency only when periods/identities support the sum, never a “subscription tax” as verified waste. Detailed subscription audit can continue in the separately available renewal workflow; no wallet history or automatic cancellation is implied.
- **Warranty:** require applicable product/policy, purchase or coverage-start evidence, stated term and the user's actual defect/remedy. Being inside a stated term alone does not establish claim eligibility. If there is no defect or policy basis, offer a document-review question rather than fabricate a warranty opportunity. One selected claim then follows this skill's existing ledger/draft workflow.

## Review before acting

Order the queue by supported deadlines and the user's priorities; show unresolved deadlines separately. Compare monetary candidates only within the same currency and compatible basis. Do not invent recovery probabilities, success rates, effort prices or expected-value rankings. If the user supplies assumptions for a hypothetical comparison, label them user assumptions rather than merchant evidence or guaranteed recovery.

Each row is `candidate`, `needs_review`, `outside_stated_window`, `not_applicable` or `evidence_incomplete`, with the exact basis and smallest next question. Never infer statutory rights or that a merchant will accept a claim. Before a mailbox draft, the user selects one order/account, purpose, remedy and exact recipient under the compose owner's contract. Reuse one matching draft, preserve edits and leave it unsent; any send is separately approved. No merchant return submission, claim link navigation, cancellation, wallet effect, automatic schedule or recurring search is introduced.
