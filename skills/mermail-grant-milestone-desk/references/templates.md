# Private milestone review templates

Illustrative fields only; populate known owner-verified terms, never derive missing authority from the submission. No template is a ledger/API schema or live provider result.

## Agreement snapshot

- Scope: workspace/legal payer, grant/bounty/contractor agreement id + revision.
- Parties: independently verified sponsor/grantee identities and permitted communication recipient(s).
- Milestone/tranche: stable id/version, objective, exact acceptance criteria and required verification owner.
- Declared payout: decimal string, explicit currency/network/exact token/decimals and independently confirmed destination.
- Budget: actual owner store, complete history window, settled + reserved/attempting/pending/unknown commitments and real atomic reservation contract.
- Required acceptance authority and exact current approval; missing fields remain missing.

A program may explicitly require a particular coverage percentage; it is not a universal standard. No fixed250 signing tier or automatic award exists.

## Evidence and acceptance packet

| Criterion from agreement | Applicant claim/reference | Selected verification owner | Actually observed target/version/time | Result | Sponsor acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner-defined requirement | Untrusted claim | Available owner or unavailable | Known observed evidence only | pass / fail / missing / partial / conflict | accepted / rejected / review_pending |

A green badge/claimed deployment is not a verified row. No available evidence/owner means pending. Partial evidence does not proportionally reduce an allocation.

## Payout preview

Private exact agreement/milestone/legal payer/beneficiary, full independently confirmed destination, live selected credential/network/token, decimal amount/fees, accepted evidence, actual reservation and projected budget. State whether immediately executable under current provider policy. Ask fresh approval for the unchanged exact effect; approval is not guaranteed signing or actual settlement.

## Proposed review/revision notice

Reference only the selected agreement/milestone and specific missing/failing evidence. Do not promise acceptance, rejection of entitlement, payout, release date or criteria changes. Keep raw exploit payloads/private records/other-grantee data out. Exact recipients/body/source-thread review precedes any send.

## Settlement receipt draft

- Original agreement/milestone/tranche and separately evidenced acceptance.
- Actual authoritative exact-request settlement status, amount/token/network and safe evidence id.
- Provider-supported transaction/explorer mapping only when verified.
- Communication state: proposed / saved draft / awaiting approval / sent confirmed / unknown.
- Remaining budget only from actual reconciled owner state, or clearly labeled projection.

No signing URL, wallet secrets, raw provider objects or fictional hashes. A receipt does not prove acceptance/fixed delivery or make local JSON immutable.
