# Proposed grant milestone desk

Review grant, bounty and contractor milestone submissions against an independently approved agreement. Separate selected read-only commit/PR/CI/deployment observations from sponsor acceptance. Draft revision or factual settlement mail for exact approval; filing is separately requested.

No new MCP tools, API, database or release version. No-wallet intake remains useful. Every amount needs fresh exact approval and real durable milestone/budget coordination; absent that, payment is a manual handoff.

## Preservation and attribution

Preserves useful scope from [#455](https://github.com/Nudgen-Marketing/mermail-skills/pull/455) (selected commit/deployment evidence) and [#242](https://github.com/Nudgen-Marketing/mermail-skills/pull/242) (agreement-defined contractor/grant tranches). Original contribution history is retained; no merge or closure is claimed.

## Tests and limits

`npm test` runs the existing validator, pure criterion/decimal projections and fork-workflow tests. Fixtures cannot establish real provenance, identity, acceptance, reservation, settlement or delivery. Helper outputs authorize none of those effects.

Demo is fictional/offline, not live end-to-end proof. Actual selected-owner adapters, current provider contracts, durable atomic coordination and approved live rehearsal remain integration prerequisites. No production or no-edge-case guarantee.
